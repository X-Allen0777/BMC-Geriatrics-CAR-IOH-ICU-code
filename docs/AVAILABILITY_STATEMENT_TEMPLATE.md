## After GitHub + Zenodo are ready

INSPIRE is available through PhysioNet to credentialed users who complete the required training and sign the data-use agreement (version 1.4.2: doi 10.13026/1eay-yc85; https://physionet.org/content/inspire/1.4.2/). Patient-level records cannot be redistributed by the authors. Aggregate supplementary results accompany this manuscript. The complete extraction, cleaning, cohort-construction, modelling, and table-generation code is publicly available at https://github.com/ORG/REPO (release v1.0.0; archived at Zenodo, doi: 10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX).
